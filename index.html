Listen to Our Planet.

What if Earth could make its own music? EarthSong transforms environmental information into sound, allowing us to experience our planet in a completely different way.

Science meets sound.

From ocean temperatures to changing forests, Earth's data tells stories. EarthSong aims to turn those stories into soundscapes that everyone can explore and understand.
